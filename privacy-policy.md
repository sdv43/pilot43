# Privacy Policy for Pilot43 Chrome Extension

Pilot43 ("we", "us", or "our") is developed and operated by Dmitriy Selivestrov. The Extension's source code and project page are available at https://github.com/sdv43/pilot43. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you install or use the Extension, how we use and share that information, and the choices you have regarding that information.

If you choose to install or use the Extension, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. The information we collect is used to provide, maintain, and improve the Extension and related services. We will not sell your personal information.

Effective date: 2026-09-21

## Information We Collect

- Information you provide directly: If you contact us (for example, for support or feedback) we may collect your name, email address and any other information you choose to provide.
- Content you choose to process: The Extension may allow you to select or submit text, page content, or other information from web pages for processing. We access page content only when you explicitly enable features that require such access. If you configure a third-party provider to process your content, the content will be transmitted to that provider.
- Chrome storage and local data: The Extension stores settings and small amounts of data locally using the Chrome Storage API (e.g., `chrome.storage.local` or `chrome.storage.sync`). This data remains on your device or is synced to your Google account if you use Chrome sync.
- Usage and log data: The Extension does not collect or transmit usage statistics, crash reports, or telemetry to us.
- Device and technical information: The Extension may read technical information (browser type and version, operating system, language) to operate correctly; this information is not transmitted to us.

## How We Use Information

- To provide and maintain the Extension and its features.
- To process content you submit (for example, sending selected text to a remote API if you enable that feature and select a provider).
- To respond to issues and discussions in the project's GitHub repository and to post important notices and updates.
- To detect, prevent and address technical issues, abuse or security incidents.

## Third-Party Services and Sharing

We may share information with third-party service providers that perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, crash reporting, and remote processing. Importantly, Pilot43 does not maintain an exhaustive list of third‑party AI or cloud providers — users may configure and supply the providers they wish to use in the Extension settings.

If you configure a third‑party provider to process your content (for example an AI or model API), the content you submit will be transmitted to that provider and processed according to the provider's privacy policy. Examples of providers commonly configured by users include AI model APIs and cloud processing services (for example, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Ollama), but this is not an exhaustive list.

We will not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

## Permissions and Access

The Extension requests only the permissions necessary to deliver its features. Pilot43 requests host permissions to access web pages so page-level features can work across sites (the extension may request the equivalent of the `<all_urls>` host permission). Chrome will show these permissions during installation or when permissions change.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Extension itself does not use browser cookies. However, third-party services you choose to use may use cookies or similar technologies. If the Extension opens web pages or embeds third-party content, those pages may use cookies according to their own policies.

## Storage, Security and Retention

Data stored locally by the Extension remains on your device unless you choose to sync it. If you transmit data to third-party providers, the retention and security of that data are governed by the provider's policies. The Extension does not maintain a central, permanent database of submitted content.

We take commercially reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Children's Privacy

We do not recommend that children under 13 use the Extension. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Continued use of the Extension after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
